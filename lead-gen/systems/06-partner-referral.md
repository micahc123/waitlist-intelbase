# 06 - Partner and Referral Program (GROW-07)

> A partner / referral program to white-label and resell intelbase OS through web agencies, dev shops,
> and consultants. Ideal partner profile, the offer and revenue-share structure options, outreach copy
> to recruit partners, a simple tracking approach, and press-go steps. This turns other people's
> client bases into a booked-call pipeline for intelbase. No em dashes. Nothing here was signed or
> sent; it is build-to-press-go.

## 1. Why this exists

Web agencies, dev shops, and consultants already have the exact clients intelbase OS is for, and they
already have the trust. They are also under pressure to add AI to their offering but cannot build it.
intelbase gives them a finished, brandable AI OS to sell; they give intelbase distribution into client
bases it would otherwise have to reach one cold email at a time. One good partner can be worth a month
of outbound. This is leverage, not volume.

It also gives the **reactivation** system (`02-reactivation.md`) a second ask: past clients who do not
want the OS for themselves may happily refer it or partner.

## 2. Ideal partner profile

| Attribute | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Web/design agencies, dev shops, marketing agencies, fractional CMOs/CTOs, business consultants, IT service providers, and bookkeepers/operations consultants who serve SMBs. |
| **Client base** | Serves the intelbase ICP: small-to-mid B2B services, agencies, and SaaS (2–50 staff) that sell via booked calls. Overlap with `../ICP_AND_APOLLO_SEARCH.md` is the point. |
| **Geography** | HK + APAC first (shared market, easy collaboration), then English markets. |
| **Signal of fit** | Already gets asked "can you add AI / a chatbot / lead gen?" by their clients and has no good answer. Builds sites or runs marketing but does not build AI. |
| **Size** | Small enough to move fast and care about a revenue share; established enough to have real clients. Solo consultants with a strong client list count. |
| **Anti-fit** | Direct competitors (other AI-agent/AI-OS sellers), and shops with no client base (nothing to refer). |

Best first partners: people intelbase already knows (warm network, past clients from
`02-reactivation.md`). Recruit warm before cold.

## 3. The offer and revenue-share structure (options)

Offer two clear tracks so a partner can pick how involved they want to be. Numbers below are
starting-point ranges to negotiate, not fixed; confirm what works for intelbase's margins.

### Track 1 - Referral (light, partner just introduces)
- Partner sends a warm intro or referral; intelbase sells, builds, and supports.
- **Structure options:**
  - **One-time finder fee:** a flat referral fee per closed client (for example a fixed amount, or
    ~10–15% of the setup fee), paid once on close.
  - **Recurring share:** ~10% of the monthly retainer for the first 6 to 12 months, so the partner
    earns while the client stays. Often the stronger motivator.
- **Best for:** consultants and contacts who just want to point clients your way without doing work.

### Track 2 - White-label / reseller (heavier, partner owns the relationship)
- Partner sells intelbase OS under their own brand (or co-branded), intelbase builds and runs it in
  the background. Partner owns the client relationship and billing.
- **Structure options:**
  - **Wholesale / markup:** intelbase charges the partner a wholesale price (setup + monthly); the
    partner marks it up to their client and keeps the spread. Clean and scalable.
  - **Revenue share:** the partner keeps ~20–30% of setup and ~20–30% of the monthly retainer; intelbase
    keeps the rest and does delivery. Negotiate by how much the partner does (sales only vs sales +
    first-line support).
- **Best for:** agencies and dev shops that want AI in their lineup without building it, and want to
  own their client.

### Guardrails on the deal
- **Protect delivery quality and the guardrail story.** Even white-labeled, the OS keeps its
  never-invent-a-price guardrail; do not let a partner over-promise on intelbase's behalf.
- **Clear scope of who does what** (sales, build, support, billing) in a one-page partner agreement.
- **No exclusivity at the start.** Keep it non-exclusive until a partner proves volume.
- **Attribution is explicit** (section 5) so revenue share is never disputed.

## 5. Tracking approach (simple)

Keep it low-tech at launch; do not buy partner software.

- **Unique referral identifier per partner:** give each partner a UTM and/or a dedicated Cal.com
  booking link, for example `{{booking_link}}?utm_source=partner&utm_campaign=<partnername>`. Any call
  or client that comes through it is attributed to that partner.
- **A partner tracking sheet** (one row per partner): partner name, track (referral vs white-label),
  agreed share, referral link/UTM, leads referred, calls booked, clients closed, revenue, share owed,
  share paid. This is the source of truth for payouts.
- **CRM tag:** `source = partner` plus the partner name on every referred lead, so partner-sourced
  booked calls show up in the one dashboard alongside the other channels.
- **Payout cadence:** monthly reconciliation from the sheet (recurring shares) and on-close for
  one-time fees. Keep it transparent so partners trust it and keep referring.
- **Upgrade later only if volume justifies it:** if you get many partners, a referral/affiliate tool
  (for example Rewardful, PartnerStack, or a Tolt-style tool) automates tracking and payouts. Defer
  until the sheet is genuinely too much. Confirm cost at that point; keep within the budget ceiling.

## 6. Routing booked calls into the same funnel

- Partner referrals book onto the **same Cal.com event** as every channel (`../BOOKING_AND_CRM.md`),
  via the partner's unique link/UTM.
- Tag `source = partner` + partner name in the master CRM.
- Booked calls from partners count in the one dashboard. The partner sheet handles the revenue-share
  layer on top.
- For white-label, the client relationship sits with the partner, but the intro call and the build
  still flow through intelbase's calendar and delivery.

## 7. PRESS GO steps

Nothing below was executed.

1. **Pick the structure** you will offer (referral fee and/or recurring share for Track 1; wholesale
   and/or revenue share for Track 2). Set the numbers against intelbase's margins.
2. **Write a one-page partner agreement** covering track, share, who-does-what (sales/build/support/
   billing), non-exclusivity, and the guardrail/quality commitment.
3. **Build the partner tracking sheet** (section 5) and a per-partner UTM / Cal.com link scheme.
4. **List warm targets first:** known agencies, dev shops, consultants, and past clients (from
   `02-reactivation.md`). Then build a small cold list from the same ICP geography.
5. **Send warm outreach** (section 4) to known contacts; run the 3-step cold sequence to the cold
   list (a warm mailbox or LinkedIn, not the cold-email infrastructure, since these are relationship
   asks).
6. **Onboard the first partners:** give them their link/UTM, a one-pager on intelbase OS they can show
   clients, and the agreement. Add them to the tracking sheet.
7. **Reconcile and pay monthly** from the sheet so partners keep referring.

**Press-go gate:** do not promise a revenue share you have not checked against margins, and have the
one-page agreement and tracking link ready before you recruit, so the first partner has a clean
experience.
