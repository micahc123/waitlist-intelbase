# Sequence 1 - Agencies and B2B services (HK + APAC)

> For Apollo Saved Search A. Buyer: founder / head of growth at a small agency or B2B services firm.
> Angle: your front office runs itself; the calendar fills without a sales hire. Dogfood proof leads.
> 5 steps over 14 business days. Stops on any reply. No links in step 1. No em dashes.

---

## Step 1 - Day 0 - Opener

**Subject:** quick one, {{first_name}}

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

Most {{industry}} teams your size lose leads in two places: visitors who land on the site and never
get answered, and outbound that nobody has time to run.

Worth a quick look for {{company}}?

{{sender_name}}

## Notes for the operator

- Step 1 has **no link** by design (cold opener deliverability). The booking link first appears in
  step 4.
- Subjects are lowercase and short on purpose; they read like a real person, not a campaign.
- If `{{signal}}` is available for a contact (for example "saw you're hiring for growth"), you may
  swap the first line of step 1 to: "Saw {{company}} is {{signal}} - most teams at that point try to
  hire the pipeline. There is another way." Keep a non-signal fallback for everyone else.
