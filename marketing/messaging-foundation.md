## Product name

**intelbase OS** — the autonomous AI operating system for your business.

(Use "intelbase OS" as the product; "intelbase" remains the company. One name everywhere — no
competing labels like "Revenue OS" / "Ops OS" in public copy.)

## One-liner (POS-01)

**intelbase OS is an AI operating system that runs your business's front office and growth on its
own — it answers every visitor, qualifies every lead, books your calls, nurtures follow-ups, and
runs your ads, with guardrails so it never goes off-script.**

## Hero (POS-01, POS-03) — primary direction

- **Headline:** An AI operating system that runs your front office — **autonomously**.
- **Sub-headline:** intelbase OS answers every visitor, qualifies every lead, books your calls,
  and runs your ads — on its own. You watch the whole thing work on one dashboard.
- **Primary CTA:** Book a free call → (single CTA)
- **Eyebrow/badge:** "Autonomous, with guardrails" / spots-open badge retained.

## The capability set (POS-03) — becomes Services

One OS, five things it runs autonomously:

1. **AI Website Concierge** — Answers visitors, qualifies them, and books calls 24/7. No forms left
   unanswered, no lead lost at midnight.
2. **Autonomous Lead Generation** — Finds your buyers and reaches out for you, filling the calendar
   with qualified calls (Apollo-powered outbound).
3. **Lead Nurture on Autopilot** — Follows up across channels until a lead books or opts out —
   without you remembering to.
4. **AI Ad Engine** — Generates the creative and runs the campaigns, optimizing spend on its own.
5. **One Control Dashboard** — The whole autonomous loop in one view: conversations, qualified
   leads, booked calls, ad performance, ROI.

## Trust / guardrails angle (POS-04) — the differentiator

**Autonomous, not reckless.** intelbase OS knows what it doesn't know. It will never invent a price,
make a promise it can't keep, or guess on something that matters — the moment it's unsure, it hands
off to you. You get full autonomy with a safety rail, not a black box.

(This directly answers the market's #1 objection to customer-facing AI — over-promising / hallucination
— surfaced in the research. Lead with it; it's how "fully autonomous" becomes credible.)

## Process (POS, feeds SITE-03)

How a business gets onto intelbase OS — and how fast it goes autonomous:

1. **Map** — We learn your business, offers, and rules (what the OS can and can't say).
2. **Build** — We configure the OS on your stack and wire it to your site, ads, and calendar.
3. **Go live, autonomously** — It runs on its own. You watch the dashboard; we tune the guardrails.

## Proof framing (feeds SITE-04 — no fabricated hard numbers)

- Lead with the **dogfood proof**: "The same OS runs on this site — the chat you're talking to *is*
  the product." (Strongest, most honest proof a small agency has.)
- KPIs as ranges/mechanics, not invented absolutes: "Every visitor answered in seconds," "Calls
  booked while you sleep," "30–50 ad variations tested per month."
- Testimonials: reframe to the autonomy benefit (front office that runs itself, calendar fills
  without a sales hire). Use ranges; swap in real quotes before go-live.

## Pricing framing (feeds SITE-05; ranges from research)

Setup + monthly retainer, quote after the call. Three tiers, no public hard prices:

- **Launch** — Core OS live on your site (Concierge + dashboard). *Setup from a one-time fee, then monthly.*
- **Growth** *(Most popular / default)* — Full OS: Concierge + autonomous lead gen + nurture + ads + dashboard.
- **Custom** — Multi-brand / bespoke rules / deeper integrations.

Research anchors (internal, not public): setup ~US$2.5k–15k; monthly ~US$0.5k–5k. Position Growth
as the default.
